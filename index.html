Store Policies

QUIT MUGGIN' • Please read before you order

Made to order

Everything we sell is printed on demand just for you, so we never sit on dead stock. Production starts after your order is placed.

Shipping & delivery

  • Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days.
  • FREE shipping on all orders over $50. Wrapping paper pre-sale orders ship free with 3 or more papers.
  • Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, holidays, and carrier delays can add time, and drop-day and holiday orders may take longer.
  • We ship within the United States. Please double-check your address at checkout.

Wrong address

We can't be responsible for packages sent to an incorrect or incomplete address entered at checkout. If you spot a mistake, contact us right away; once an order is in production the address may not be changeable.

Refunds & replacements (photo proof required)

  • If your item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery.
  • Include your order number and clear photos of the item, the packaging, and the shipping label. For broken mugs, please show the damage and how it was packed.
  • Once approved, we'll send a free replacement or issue a refund to your original payment method.
  • Refunds can take up to 2–5 business days to show up, depending on your bank institution.

What we can't refund

  • Change of mind, or items ordered in the wrong size, color, or phone model. Please check the size details before you order.
  • Minor color differences between your screen and the printed item.
  • Damage caused by misuse, wear, or improper care after delivery.
  • Orders delivered to the address you provided, or reports made after 14 days.

Returns

Because items are made just for you, we don't accept returns for a change of mind. Please don't send anything back before contacting us, as unapproved returns can't be accepted or refunded.

Cancellations & changes

You can request a cancellation or change within 24 hours of ordering, as long as production hasn't started. After that, the order can't be changed or cancelled.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows no movement for 2 or more business days, or it says delivered but you can't find it, check with neighbors and your local carrier first, then contact us and we'll help sort it out as soon as possible.

Pre-sale orders

Pre-sale orders are reserved ahead of the public drop and are made to order, so they follow the same shipping, refund, and cancellation terms above. The deal (Buy 2, Get 1 For 50% Off + FREE Shipping) applies automatically at checkout, and the 50% off goes to the 3rd paper in your order. Pre-sale customers get early access to the drop through their client portal.

Pricing & availability

We do our best to keep every listing accurate. If a price or product detail is wrong, we may correct it or cancel and refund the affected order. Drops are limited and designs may sell out or retire at any time.

Privacy

We only use your name, email, phone, and address to fulfill and support your order. We never sell your information.

Contact us

Have an issue with an order? Reach out with your order number and we'll make it right.
